DATA & LIMITS
  • Spatial Truncation: The study box clips ~32% of the western reservoir arms at 76.450°E. All stats reflect the eastern basin study box (~60% of FRL).
  • Sparse Temporal Sampling: Only 3 NISAR Track 12 GCOV acquisitions exist across 2025–2026.
  • Threshold Sensitivity: Calibrated fixed threshold (-16.0 dB, HH). Wind-induced capillary waves or rain can elevate water backscatter causing omission error.
  • Validation Ground Truth: Confirmed monotonic agreement with official KSNDMC reservoir gauge levels (123.6 ft > 108.7 ft > 98.5 ft).
  • Dam Embankment Artifact: The ~1.19 km² gain near the dam is influenced by radar double-bounce layover on steep masonry structures.
