Physical SAR Insight: Calm water creates specular radar reflection (dark pixels, <-16 dB). During the August monsoon, surface capillary waves induced by monsoon winds increase backscatter to -16.2 dB. Our calibrated -16.0 dB threshold captures the contiguous reservoir without land bleeding.
Honest Uncertainty & Clipping: The KRS reservoir extends west to ~76.35°E. Because our NISAR frame starts at 76.45°E, ~32% of the western inflow tail is clipped. All numbers reflect the eastern basin study box (~60% of total FRL).
