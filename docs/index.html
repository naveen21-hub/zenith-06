DATA & LIMITS
  • Spatial Truncation: The study box clips ~40% of the full reservoir water-spread area (~45% of linear E-W reach west of 76.450°E), capturing ~60% of FRL (77.93 km² of ~129.5 km² published FRL spread). All stats apply strictly within the study box.
  • August Surface Backscatter Sensitivity (Hypothesis): A 4.92 km² open-water patch near the dam is detected in Nov '25 and Nov '26 but missed in Aug '26 due to +1.1 to +1.2 dB brighter backscatter (median -15.68 dB vs -16.93 dB), placing 75% of patch pixels in the -16 to -14 dB range. This is hypothesized to result from monsoonal surface roughness (wind capillary waves or rain agitation), creating threshold omission without coincident meteorological verification.
  • Acquisitions Analysed: Three acquisitions analysed (23 Nov 2025, 14 Aug 2026, 06 Nov 2026). In the ASF archive, 16 Track 12 GCOV granules exist for this frame across the 12-day repeat orbit: Cycles 006–010 (23 Nov 2025, 05 Dec 2025, 17 Dec 2025, 29 Dec 2025, 10 Jan 2026) and Cycles 024–035 (27 Jun, 09 Jul, 21 Jul, 14 Aug, 26 Aug, 07 Sep, 19 Sep, 01 Oct, 13 Oct, 25 Oct, 06 Nov 2026).
  • Threshold Sensitivity: Fixed threshold (-16.0 dB, HH). Monsoonal waves or rain can elevate water backscatter causing omission error.
  • Validation Ground Truth: Consistent in direction with approximate reported levels (news reports, exact-date official readings not found).
  • Dam Downstream Cutoff: All masks are clipped to the reservoir side of the dam wall, excluding 0.95 km² of downstream irrigation canals and flooded paddy fields.
